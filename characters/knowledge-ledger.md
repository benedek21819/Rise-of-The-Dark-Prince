# Knowledge Ledger — who knows what, and when they learned it

**Purpose.** The single most frequent continuity error in this book is not characterization or worldbuilding — it is **a character knowing something they have no way of knowing yet**, or being made to ask about something they already know. This file exists to be checked before drafting any scene with two or more people in it.

**Current story position: end of Ch.76, Day 25 of the war count.** Ch.77 jumps forward to Thaw.

**How to use it.** Read Part One before writing a scene where a secret is in the room. Read a character's entry in Part Two before writing their dialogue. **Update this file whenever a chapter moves a fact from one column to another** — that is the whole discipline, and it takes a minute.

**`OPEN` means genuinely undecided**, not forgotten. Do not resolve an OPEN item in prose without deciding it here first.

## Part One — The secrets register

### S1. Morvath is alive
| | |
|---|---|
| **Knew before Day 20** | Vesper. Orlena. Dain. **Four of the keepers.** King Aldric — since Vesper was twenty, lawfully reported under the law requiring every dragon be declared to the crown, and never published. Lirath. |
| **Learned on Day 20** | Lucian. Selene (identified him from the long gallery book before anyone told her). Elara. Valtor. Six Silver Guard on the platforms and three on the second bridge. **Much of Firsthold**, which spent the morning looking up. **Roderic**, who fought that war. And roughly **four thousand dispersing Dravenhall soldiers** now walking the length of Kaelor with the story. |
| **Lucian's full account** | Day 22, from Vesper on the eastern scree (Ch.75): the duel, the sea, the shore, the deep cave, that **Morvath and Lirath are mates**, and that Vesper knew **through Lirath**. |
| **Nobody knows** | What Aldric will do about it. Vesper's letter (Day 23) reported the sighting and did not ask. |
| **OPEN** | Whether **Riven and Thorne** saw him. They were in the valley overnight Day 20–21. Decide before either speaks of it. |

**Vesper's stated position (Ch.74):** the house explains itself to nobody, the story is already gone, and it will come back in a year changed and worse.

### S2. Lucian's latent capacity for Commandments
**Known to nobody at all** — not Lucian, not Vesper, not Orlena. Congenital, has appeared in House Caldarion before. **Do not let anyone suspect it.**

### S3. Commandments generally
Vesper learned they exist **at succession** — the Vaults open only to the head of house. **Vesper does not carry one.** He recognized what took Lirath (Ch.71) by inference and inheritance, not by possessing the gift.

### S4. The Absence costing Lucian nothing
Lucian knows it happened — twice, under real pressure (Ch.68, Ch.72). He has **no explanation and has not raised it with anyone.**
**OPEN, and important:** whether Vesper knows Lucian used it at all. Decide before any scene where it could come up.

### S5. The Primordial Darkness, and what is in the Vaults
Vesper knows. **Lucian does not, and does not learn it until after the Sisters arrive**, in the Vaults (`magic-and-power-system.md`). Ch.68 and Ch.72 plant the anomaly only. **No character may gesture at this before then.**

### S6. House Kaelarion is technically vassal to House Caldarion
**Only the current heads of both houses know:** Vesper and Aldric. Not Lucian. Not Seren. Not Dorian. Not Isolde.

### S7. The sphere, and Myrath

### S8. Isolde informed on her own brother

### S9. Whether Lirath will ever fly again
**Nobody knows.** Not the physician, not the keepers, not Vesper. The wing was under her for eighteen hours and she would not shift.
**Except possibly Thorne**, who had his hands on her at dawn on Day 21, saw whatever there was to see, carried it five hundred feet up a path and has not looked up since. **He has never said. Do not make him say.**

### S10. Ancalion's healing
Lucian knew he would live **through the bond**, in the early hours of Day 21, before anyone said it aloud. His **exceptional** rate of healing is true (`magic-and-power-system.md`) but **is not to be stated on the page yet** — too little time has passed for anyone to have noticed.

## Part Two — By character

### Lucian (18)
**Knows:** everything in S1 as of Day 22. The war's full count. That the object came off Korrath's harness and what it does. That Myrath made it and it cannot be proven. That his father has handed him the ordinary running of the house (Ch.75) and keeps only what would change it.
**Does not know:** S2, S5, S6. What is in Aldric's reply. Whether Lirath will fly. Whether Ancalion heals faster than other dragons. What Aldric decides about Morvath.
**Carries unexplained:** the Absence costing him nothing. He has not asked anyone.

### Vesper
**Knows:** S1 (since he was twenty), S3, S5, S6, S7. Firsthold's count in full — he sent it south unsoftened and unrounded on purpose.
**Does not know:** S2. Whether Lirath will fly.
**OPEN:** S4 and S8 — whether he knows about the Absence, and whether he knows about Isolde.

### Roderic
**Knows:** S1 (recognized Morvath on Day 20). S7 in full. That Maren betrayed him. That Korrath is dead — he felt it go, after thirty years.
**Does not know:** whether anything he gave them has produced a single consequence. **Four months and nobody has said the word Myrath to him, or his sister's name.** He has no idea whether either landed. *This is the cell's real torture and it costs Caldarion nothing.*
**Will know by Ch.77:** the terms — held at Blackspire until he can be moved, court sits in spring.

### Seren (15)
**Knows, as of Day 26:** everything in Lucian's letter — the siege, the forty thousand, both days, Lirath down and alive, Ancalion torn and mending, the scar on his face, and that Roderic is alive in a cell and coming south to her father.
**Does not know:** S1, S2, S5, S6. Anything about the sphere.
**Note:** her own letter crossed his on the road. She wrote it knowing nothing.
**OPEN:** whether her Veilsight has manifested yet on-page. Her character file describes her as a Veilseer, but the manifestation is the trigger for the Sisters' arrival — **decide the timing before writing her again.**

### Aldric
**Knows:** S1, lawfully, with a record, since Vesper was twenty — and has never been asked to defend choosing not to publish it. S6. S8. The three strands of evidence against Dravenhall.
**Does not know:** S7 — nothing about the sphere or Myrath.
**Has, as of Day 26:** Vesper's letter, Firsthold's count in full, and the fact that a third dragon was seen by a garrison, a city and the enemy.

### Isolde
**Knows:** S8 — her own part in it, taken deliberately with her eyes open. That she would be the first name Roderic reached for.
**Does not know:** S1, S6, S7.
**Does not repent, and is not to be written repenting.** She acted for the crown, which is her son's, and she has never forgiven the war her father lost or loved the house whose strength decided it.

### Selene (~12)
Watched the whole of Day 20 from the eastern gallery. **Identified Morvath herself** from the long gallery book, in three separate places, before anybody told her. Knows he is staying down there and that Lucian did not know about him beforehand.

### Elara, Dain, Orlena
**Orlena and Dain** are among the handful who have known S1 all along. **Elara** learned it on Day 20 with everyone else and went very still by the fire when Selene said it aloud. None of the three has said anything further about it on the page.
