# King Aldric Kaelarion

**Full Name:** King Aldric Kaelarion  
**Titles:** King of the Kingdom, Head of House Kaelarion  
**Birth Year:** 1006 IE  
**Age:** Middle-aged (49 as of the main story's present, ≈1055 IE)

**Appearance:** Dignified and regal, with the bearing of a long-time ruler. Kaelarion gold hair, now heavily greying, and blue eyes. He carries visible signs of weariness and responsibility — subtle lines of stress and a quieter presence than in his younger years. He dresses in the royal colors of deep crimson and gold.

**Personality:** The Weary Stabilizer — a tired but responsible ruler who prioritizes peace and stability above all else. He is genuinely grateful to House Caldarion for their role in securing the throne and believes the alliance is the only thing preventing another devastating war. He is frustrated by both his wife Isolde’s ambition and his son Dorian’s impatience, but he is often too cautious to confront them openly. His greatest strength is his ability to keep fragile alliances intact, even if it means accepting uncomfortable compromises.

**Strengths:** Political experience, long-term thinking, ability to maintain fragile alliances, genuine concern for the kingdom’s stability.
**Flaws:** Overly cautious, sometimes passive, struggles to control the ambitions within his own family.

**Abilities:** Bonded to the royal dragon **Solara** (bright golden scales, white eyes, crimson flames). Moderate experience in leadership and statecraft rather than personal combat prowess.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Queen Isolde Kaelarion** (first wife): Respects her intelligence but is frustrated by her more aggressive political ambitions regarding House Caldarion. Married her ≈1032 as a deliberate reconciliation match with House Dravenhall, and has never fully stopped wondering how much of her remains her father's daughter beneath the crown she now wears.
- **Lady Maris Kaelarion** (second wife): Married her ≈1043 to consolidate House Vaelmar's naval strength and coastal watch more tightly to the throne. A simpler, less freighted marriage than his first — she wants little from him beyond honesty, which he finds restful after a decade of Isolde's careful calculations.
- **Crown Prince Dorian Kaelarion** (elder son, by Isolde): Strained relationship. He is disappointed by Dorian’s jealousy and impatience, and struggles to guide him toward more responsible leadership.
- **Prince Corvin Kaelarion** (younger son, by Isolde): An easier relationship than the one he has with Dorian — Corvin asks less of him and gives him less to manage, which Aldric recognizes, with some private guilt, as part of why he enjoys his company more.
- **Princess Seren Kaelarion** (daughter, by Isolde): Close and loving. He is protective of her and supportive of the engagement to Lucian as a means of securing lasting peace.
- **Princess Nerissa Kaelarion** (youngest daughter, by Maris): Dotes on her in the easy, undemanding way available to a fourth child with no weight of succession on her.
- **Lady Liora Kaelarion** (younger sister): Fond, easy, one of the few relationships in his life that isn't work. She married into House Sylvaron in her youth and left court politics behind her with a thoroughness he sometimes envies.
- **House Caldarion**: Views them as essential allies. He is grateful but also quietly concerned about their overwhelming military power. This gratitude is rooted in the deep, brother-like friendship between his father **Aldorion Kaelarion** and Lucian’s grandfather, who together forged the original alliance between the two houses.
- **Lord Vesper Caldarion**: Beyond the alliance, the two share a private matter from the war's aftermath — Vesper, freshly head of his own house, told the already-reigning Aldric personally when it became clear Morvath had survived. The report satisfied the law; making it public was Aldric's choice to withhold, well within his right as king. He and Vesper agreed discretion served both houses better than disclosure, and Aldric has kept it in the decades since, telling no one, not even Isolde or Dorian. In the years since, without ever having proof, the two have also quietly hedged against the possibility of a Dravenhall threat — Aldric through careful, undramatic use of the Crown's Eyes and diplomatic positioning, Vesper through Caldarion's own military readiness — on the shared, unspoken principle that being prepared and not needing it costs far less than the reverse.

**Goals:**
- Maintain peace and stability in the kingdom
- Preserve the alliance with House Caldarion without becoming completely dependent
- Secure a stable future for his children and the dynasty

**Role in Story:** A well-intentioned but weakening king whose desire for stability is constantly threatened by the ambitions of those around him (his wife, his son, and the rising power of Lucian and House Caldarion).
