# Maren Dravenhall

**Full Name:** Maren Dravenhall  
**Titles:** None of consequence — a minor cousin of the ruling line, not a claimant to the seat.  
**House:** Dravenhall (cousin branch, not direct line)  
**Age:** Roughly Isolde's own age.

**Role at Dravenhall:** Oversees a portion of the house's military administration — muster correspondence and levy records for the minor allied houses Dravenhall draws men from. A working role rather than a ceremonial one; the kind of position that leaves no obvious trace of influence but grants real, detailed access to exactly the sort of information Roderic would never think to guard against family.

**Personality:** Quiet, precise, unshowy — easy to underestimate and overlook in a house that prizes visible strength and martial glory over administrative competence. Not motivated by petty grievance or personal ambition. She has watched Roderic's buildup accumulate for years, at close range, and has arrived at her own honest conviction that he is steering the house toward a war it does not need and cannot cleanly win — closer to conscience than to resentment.

**Connection to Isolde:** Corresponded with Isolde under careful pretext in the lead-up to Ch.64/65, confirming which minor allied lords Roderic had quietly begun drawing men from, and how long the recruitment had been underway — the detail neither the financial trail nor Crown's Eyes surveillance could have supplied. Acted deliberately, understanding the risk to herself.

**Current Status (as of the Roderic POV chapter following Ch.65 "Herald"):** Identified as the likely source with enough certainty that she has to go dark and disappear before Roderic can move against her directly — not caught, not confronted on-page. Her exact fate and whereabouts after this are open story space.

**Future Potential:** Kept deliberately alive and at large (unlike the retainer/servant thread, which is treated as disposable) specifically so she can reappear later — as a continuing source, a person Isolde or the crown might need to protect, or a figure whose conscience-driven break from Dravenhall could matter more directly to the war's aftermath.
