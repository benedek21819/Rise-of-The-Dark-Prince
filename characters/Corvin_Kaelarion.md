# Prince Corvin Kaelarion

**Full Name:** Prince Corvin Kaelarion  
**Titles:** Second Prince of the Kingdom  
**Birth Year:** 1039 IE  
**Age:** 1 year younger than Dorian, 2 years older than Seren

**Appearance:** Shares his father's more than his mother's — a quieter, less immediately striking presence than Dorian, brown hair rather than Kaelarion gold, a build suited to endurance rather than display. Easy to overlook in a room until he speaks.

**Personality:** The Overlooked Second Son — calm, observant, and genuinely more capable of statecraft than the brother who will inherit ahead of him, though he has never once said so aloud and shows no visible resentment at the arrangement. Where Dorian schemes from insecurity, Corvin reasons from patience; where Dorian resents House Caldarion's power, Corvin studies it, and has quietly concluded that his brother's approach to the alliance would end badly for the crown. He keeps this conclusion to himself. He has learned, watching his mother's attention pour entirely into Dorian, that being the spare carries its own freedom — no one expects enough of him to be disappointed, which leaves him room to actually think.

**Strengths:** Genuine political judgment, patience, a talent for being underestimated, real (if quiet) diplomatic skill.
**Flaws:** Passivity — he sees the better path clearly and rarely pushes for it, both from temperament and from a learned caution about outshining his brother. Can be too willing to let bad decisions play out rather than risk the confrontation of opposing them.

**Key Relationships:**
- **King Aldric Kaelarion** (father): A quieter bond than the one Aldric has with Dorian, but a more genuinely easy one — Aldric can talk to Corvin without managing him.
- **Queen Isolde Kaelarion** (mother): Loved, but not favored, and both of them know it. Isolde's ambition has always run toward Dorian; Corvin has made peace with standing outside that current, though not without cost to how much he lets himself want from her.
- **Crown Prince Dorian Kaelarion** (elder brother): Not hostile — Corvin has no interest in the throne and Dorian knows it, which is the one thing that keeps their relationship uncomplicated. Dorian dismisses him as unambitious. Corvin lets him.
- **Princess Seren Kaelarion** (younger sister): The closest bond he has in the family. Neither of them is positioned to inherit real power, and both have found something like relief in that shared position.
- **House Caldarion**: Studies them carefully rather than resenting or leaning on them reflexively. Privately believes Dorian's hostility toward the alliance is the single most dangerous instinct in the family.

**Role in Story:** A quiet counterweight to Dorian within the royal family — proof that competence and ambition don't have to travel together, and a future source of real tension if the crown's succession is ever seriously questioned. Useful as a more reasonable royal voice Lucian or Seren could plausibly deal with directly, and as a long-term question mark: what happens if the kingdom eventually needs the better king rather than the one already in line.
