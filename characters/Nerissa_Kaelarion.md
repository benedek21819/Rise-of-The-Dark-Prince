# Princess Nerissa Kaelarion

**Full Name:** Princess Nerissa Kaelarion  
**Titles:** Princess of the Kingdom (daughter of King Aldric Kaelarion and Lady Maris)  
**Birth Year:** 1044 IE  
**Age:** Youngest of Aldric's children — 7 years younger than Seren

**Appearance:** Favors her mother more than her father — dark hair, quick eyes, already restless in the way Maris describes as "wanting the deck under her feet" long before she has any real notion of what that means. Still young enough that her place in the family's formal portraits is more a matter of arrangement than presence.

**Personality:** Too young yet for a fully settled character, but already showing her mother's directness — impatient with court ceremony, drawn to open spaces and the palace's river-facing terraces over its inner halls. Where Seren's warmth is deliberate and chosen, Nerissa's restlessness looks inherited rather than decided, though it is far too early to know what shape it will take as she grows.

**Strengths:** Nothing yet proven — still a child. What can already be seen: a fearlessness around water and height that unsettles her nursemaids, and an ear for direct speech that makes her, even now, oddly hard to lie to.
**Flaws:** Too young to assess meaningfully.

**Key Relationships:**
- **King Aldric Kaelarion** (father): Dotes on her in the easy, undemanding way available to a fourth child with no weight of succession on her — a relationship with none of the strain that marks his bond with Dorian.
- **Lady Maris Kaelarion** (mother): Close and immediate; Maris is raising her deliberately outside the court's slower rhythms, with regular trips back toward Vaelmar's coast already planned for when she's a little older.
- **Queen Isolde Kaelarion**: Polite distance, inherited from the same formal hierarchy that sits between their mothers. Too young for real rivalry with Dorian, Corvin, or Seren to have formed yet.
- **Dorian, Corvin, Seren** (half-siblings): Barely known to any of them yet, given the age gap — a name and a face at family occasions more than a real relationship so far.

**Role in Story:** Currently a background fact of the royal family rather than an active character — establishes House Vaelmar's tie to the crown through blood and leaves room to develop her later, whether as a genuine character in her own right (a princess raised toward the sea rather than the throne) or as a quiet future asset if the mainland-facing thread of the story ever needs a royal connection to Vaelmar specifically.
