# Lord Marrick Sylvaron

**Full Name:** Lord Marrick Sylvaron  
**Titles:** Head of House Sylvaron, Lord of the Golden Lowlands  
**Affiliation:** House Sylvaron  
**Relation:** Husband of Lady Liora Sylvaron (née Kaelarion), the King's sister  
**Status:** Alive

**Concept:** A banker who built an empire on not asking questions, now unknowingly exposed by exactly that habit  
Wealthy, pragmatic, and opportunistic in the way every Sylvaron before him has been — his house's whole model of power runs on volume, discretion, and never scrutinizing a profitable account too closely. That same discretion is precisely how House Dravenhall's deniable coin-trail has been able to move through Sylvaron's vaults, funding a proxy war in Caldarion's Northern Duchy, without Marrick ever consciously choosing to be part of it.

**Appearance:**  
Comfortable rather than martial — well-dressed in Sylvaron's gold and green without ostentation, the look of a man who has never once needed to prove his strength with his body. Carries himself with the easy confidence of someone who has never lost a negotiation that mattered.

**Personality:**  
Shrewd, personable, and genuinely uninterested in conflict for its own sake — Sylvaron's rivalry with Caldarion is economic and political to him, not personal, and he would far rather out-maneuver a rival quietly than fight one openly. Not a villain by his own understanding of himself; simply a man who has built his entire fortune on the principle that asking too many questions about where money comes from is bad for business. That principle has left a blind spot he does not know exists.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Lady Liora Sylvaron**: Wife, and House Sylvaron's most valuable asset by marriage — her Kaelarion blood opens doors his own name never could. He values her genuinely, not merely politically, though their marriage began as exactly the transaction it looks like. She has never involved herself in the mechanics of his banking business, and he has never invited her to.
- **House Dravenhall**: No direct alliance or loyalty. Marrick has never knowingly done business with Roderic Dravenhall's coin specifically — the funding has moved through layers of intermediaries deniable enough that he has no idea his own vaults are involved.
- **House Caldarion**: Rivalrous in the ordinary Sylvaron way — sees them as a threat to economic interests, prefers to weaken them through trade and influence rather than open conflict. Has no idea his own house's negligence has made him an unwitting instrument in a war against them.
- **King Aldric Kaelarion**: Cordial brother-in-law relationship, more social than political. Marrick has no real influence over the Crown's decisions and has never particularly sought any.

**Role in Story:** A second, financial thread into the ongoing Dravenhall investigation, parallel to the Corvane/Harrow arc — Sylvaron's vaults as an unwitting conduit for the same deniable funding network already being traced. Not yet discovered by anyone outside Dravenhall itself, which makes him a live, undetected complication rather than a settled fact of the world. Also extends the royal family's reach into House Sylvaron without granting them any real leverage over the Crown, per Liora's own established distance from actual influence.
