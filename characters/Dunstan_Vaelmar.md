# Lord Dunstan Vaelmar

**Full Name:** Lord Dunstan Vaelmar  
**Titles:** Elder brother of Lord Ellard Vaelmar; overseer of House Vaelmar's fleets, coastal trade, and Ironwood exports  
**Affiliation:** House Vaelmar  
**Relation:** Older brother of Lord Ellard Vaelmar  
**Status:** Alive

**Concept:** The brother who lost the argument the whole house had to have  
When their father died, House Vaelmar faced a real contested succession — not a formality, but a genuine choice between two visions for the house's future: Dunstan's conservative, stability-first management of what Vaelmar still had, against Ellard's hunger to reach for what it had lost. The house chose Ellard. Most of Vaelmar wanted its old glory back badly enough to bet on the brother who promised to chase it, and Dunstan has had to live inside that choice ever since.

**Appearance:**  
Older, heavier-set than Ellard, with the same sea-worn weathering but none of his brother's straight-backed performance of pride. Dresses plainly for a lord of a Great House, more concerned with practicality than presentation.

**Personality:**  
Conservative, pragmatic, and genuinely competent — he was given real, substantial control over House Vaelmar's actual day-to-day economy (the fishing fleets, coastal trade, Ironwood exports) precisely because the house still needs his skill, whatever it decided about who should lead it. Beneath the competence sits an old, mostly-buried resentment: he still believes, quietly and without much hope of ever being proven right in a way that matters, that he was the correct choice, and that Ellard's ambition is a slow-burning risk to everything Vaelmar has left. He is not bitter enough to undermine his brother openly — family loyalty and the house's stability both matter too much to him for that — but the old rivalry surfaces in unguarded moments.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Lord Ellard Vaelmar** (younger brother): Real loyalty complicated by a contest that never fully resolved itself in either man's private thinking. Ellard won the house's confidence; Dunstan has never quite stopped believing he was owed it instead. They work together well in practice, which does not mean the old argument is settled between them.
- **Lord Joren Vaelmar** (nephew): Views him as living proof of everything he warned the house about — Ellard's ambition, inherited by the next generation and sharpened rather than tempered by time. Their relationship is cordial rather than warm.
- **Lady Meret Vaelmar** (sister-in-law): Respects her steadiness, and privately suspects she is the only reason Ellard's ambition hasn't already cost the house more than it has.

**Role in Story:** The practical, skeptical center of House Vaelmar's internal politics — proof that the house's restoration ambition was a real, contested choice rather than a universal instinct, and a standing reminder that not everyone in Vaelmar agrees with the path Ellard and Joren are set on. Useful for showing the cost and risk of that ambition from the inside, by someone with every reason to point it out and just enough restraint not to do so loudly.
