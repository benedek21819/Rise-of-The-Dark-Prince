# Lady Ilyandra Myrath

**Full Name:** Lady Ilyandra Myrath  
**Titles:** Archmagister of Oldwood, Head of House Myrath  
**Age:** Elderly, venerable — decades into her tenure as head of house

**Appearance:** Spare and severe in the way of someone who has spent a lifetime valuing precision over presentation. Grey hair kept simply bound, plain scholar's robes in Myrath's colors rather than anything ornamental. Sharp, assessing eyes that rarely miss a detail worth noting.

**Personality:** The Principled Neutral — genuinely believes that Myrath's refusal to take sides is a moral stance, not cowardice, and has spent her whole life defending that position against houses who read it as self-interest dressed in philosophy. She isn't entirely wrong, and she isn't entirely right either — Myrath's neutrality really has preserved knowledge and lives that partisanship would have cost, but it has also made the house extremely comfortable, extremely wealthy in information, and never once required it to risk anything for a cause it claimed to value. Ilyandra has never fully reckoned with that tension, and doesn't care to.

**Strengths:** Formidable scholarly authority, decades of accumulated knowledge of Kaelor's true history (as much of it as survives), absolute control over what the archives ever release and to whom.
**Flaws:** Pride dressed as principle; a tendency to treat neutrality as an excuse not to act even when action might be the right thing. Underestimates how dangerous some of what her own archives hold actually is, because she has never had reason to test it against anything real.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Magister Emeric Myrath** (nephew, liaison at the Hall of Inquiry): Trusts him with real responsibility earlier than most heads of house would, in part because his curiosity mirrors her own at his age — and hasn't yet decided whether that similarity worries her.
- **House Caldarion**: Respects their power, privately disapproves of their secrecy, and has no idea how much closer to the truth about them her own archives already sit.
- **The Crown**: Cooperative at a careful distance — Myrath answers historical and magical questions when asked, and asks nothing in return that would compromise its neutrality.

**Role in Story:** The distant authority behind House Myrath's decisions — rarely seen directly, but the reason Emeric's curiosity has room to operate at all. A long-game figure: whether she ultimately protects the prophecy fragment's secrecy or allows it to be studied properly could matter enormously later, and she has not yet had a reason to decide which.
