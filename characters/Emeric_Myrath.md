# Magister Emeric Myrath

**Full Name:** Magister Emeric Myrath  
**Titles:** House Myrath's formal liaison to the Hall of Inquiry, Aurelia  
**Age:** Young-to-middle adult — old enough to hold real responsibility, young enough that his aunt's trust in him is still being tested

**Appearance:** Unremarkable by design — plain scholar's dress, ink-stained hands, the kind of face that's easy to overlook in a room full of nobles, which suits a house that prefers to observe rather than be observed. More animated than his Myrath relatives when a subject actually interests him, which he has learned to control in public and rarely bothers to in private.

**Personality:** The Curious Liaison — genuinely more interested in finding things out than in what Myrath's doctrine says he should do once he's found them, which makes him a useful scholar and a slightly unreliable instrument of house policy. He believes, more sincerely than his aunt does, that neutrality should mean impartial truth-seeking rather than convenient silence — a distinction Ilyandra has noticed and not yet decided whether to correct. Stationed in Aurelia specifically because the Hall of Inquiry's daily operations need someone competent and personable there; his placement was practical, not a reward, though he has made more of the position than anyone expected.

**Strengths:** Genuine scholarly rigor, a talent for making people underestimate how much he's actually paying attention, real institutional access to the Hall's archives — including, eventually, its Restricted Vaults.
**Flaws:** Curiosity that outpaces caution. Has read further into some of the Vaults' more dangerous fragments than his aunt is aware of, without yet grasping how much any of it actually matters.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Lady Ilyandra Myrath** (aunt, head of house): Respects her authority, chafes quietly at how much of Myrath's neutrality he's come to see as convenience rather than principle.
- **The Hall of Inquiry**: His actual daily world — the Great Archive, the Map Room, and increasingly, the Restricted Vaults, where he has spent more unsupervised hours than the house officially knows.
- **The prophecy fragment**: Has read it. Has not yet solved it. Suspects, without proof, that it means something other than what history has always assumed — a suspicion he has not shared with anyone, including Ilyandra.
- **House Caldarion**: No direct relationship yet — a natural point of contact if Lucian, Seren, or anyone else in House Caldarion's orbit ever has reason to visit the Hall of Inquiry during time in Aurelia.

**Role in Story:** The most accessible thread into Myrath's slow-burn secret. Positioned to plausibly interact with Lucian or others during the Aurelia arc without requiring a trip to Oldwood, and carrying, unknowingly, a partial key to a truth that could unravel centuries of Caldarion secrecy if his curiosity ever outruns his caution.
