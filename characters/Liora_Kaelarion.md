# Lady Liora Kaelarion

**Full Name:** Lady Liora, née Kaelarion, now of House Sylvaron  
**Titles:** Younger sister of King Aldric Kaelarion; by marriage, Lady of the Golden Lowlands  
**Birth Year:** 1011 IE  
**Age:** 5 years younger than Aldric

**Origin:** Aldorion Kaelarion's younger child, born five years after Aldric and raised in his shadow — not unhappily. She takes after her father's charisma more visibly than Aldric does, without inheriting his idealism; where Aldorion believed alliances could be built on genuine vision, Liora has always understood them as transactions dressed in warmer language. Married into House Sylvaron in her early twenties, a match arranged for exactly the reason it looks like: House Sylvaron's wealth and trade reach in exchange for a blood tie to the crown.

**Appearance:** Bright blonde hair and the same natural regal ease that marks the Kaelarion line, worn more lightly and more knowingly than her brother wears his. Favors Sylvaron's gold and green over Kaelarion crimson these days, a small, deliberate signal of where her interests now formally sit.

**Personality:** The Practical Sister — warm in company, shrewd underneath it, entirely unsentimental about the difference between the two. She loves her brother genuinely and finds his caution mildly exhausting. She has built a comfortable, quietly powerful position for herself in the Golden Lowlands, and has no particular wish to trade it for the more exposed position her niece and nephews occupy at court. She watches the crown's affairs from a useful distance — close enough to matter, far enough to never be blamed for anything.

**Strengths:** Genuine charm, financial and trade acumen absorbed from House Sylvaron, a wide and well-tended network of informal contacts across the merchant houses.
**Flaws:** Self-interest that can shade into indifference toward problems that don't touch Sylvaron directly; prefers profitable neutrality to taking sides, even when a side might be the right one to take.

**Key Relationships:**
- **King Aldric Kaelarion** (elder brother): Fond, easy, mildly exasperated by his caution — one of the few relationships in his life that doesn't require careful management.
- **House Sylvaron**: Married in, but has become genuinely useful to them rather than merely decorative; her Kaelarion blood opens doors her husband's own name would not.
- **Queen Isolde Kaelarion**: Cordial rather than close. Liora finds Isolde's ambition on Dorian's behalf a touch naive for someone from a house that lost a war betting on strength alone.
- **Dorian, Corvin, Seren** (niece and nephews): Fond but distant — the occasional letter, the rare visit, an aunt more than a fixture.

**Role in Story:** Extends the royal family's web beyond the Caldarion/Dravenhall axis and into House Sylvaron's economic power — a resource for later political maneuvering (trade leverage, neutral ground, an informal channel of information) that doesn't carry the baked-in hostility Dravenhall does. A useful figure to introduce if the story needs a royal relative who is genuinely reasonable to deal with, for reasons of self-interest rather than principle.
