# Lady Maris Kaelarion

**Full Name:** Lady Maris, née Vaelmar, now of House Kaelarion  
**Titles:** Lady Consort of the Kingdom (second wife of King Aldric Kaelarion, ranking beneath Queen Isolde)  
**Birth Year:** 1013 IE  
**Age:** 4 years younger than Isolde

**Origin:** Born of House Vaelmar, the Eastern Marches' naval power and the kingdom's principal gateway toward Arcanor. Her marriage to Aldric (≈1043) extended the same logic that brought Isolde to court a decade earlier — not reconciliation this time, but consolidation, binding the crown's naval strength and its coastal watch on the mainland's dangers more tightly to the throne. Unlike the Dravenhall match, this one was never a matter of mending a grievance. Vaelmar had no old wound to answer for; the marriage simply made a useful house more useful still.

**Appearance:** Sun-weathered in a way court ladies rarely are, having grown up on working docks rather than garden walks. Dark hair kept practically bound rather than elaborately dressed, quick eyes, a build suited to sailing rather than standing still. Wears the royal crimson and gold over Vaelmar's blue and silver, never quite letting the second disappear beneath the first.

**Personality:** Practical, plainspoken, and largely uninterested in the layered maneuvering that consumes Isolde's attention. Vaelmar's culture values movement, risk, and direct dealing over inland court patience, and Maris has never fully adapted to — or wanted to adapt to — Aurelia's slower, more indirect rhythms. She is fond of Aldric in an uncomplicated way Isolde has never quite managed, precisely because she wants nothing from the throne beyond what her marriage already secured for her house. This makes her, without any particular effort on her part, one of the few people at court Isolde cannot easily read or predict.

**Strengths:** Genuine directness, deep knowledge of naval and coastal affairs, a straightforwardness that reads as refreshing to some at court and as dangerously naive to others.
**Flaws:** Impatient with political subtlety to the point of missing real threats hidden inside it; chafes visibly at the confinement of court life, which Isolde has occasionally used against her.

**Key Relationships:**
- **King Aldric Kaelarion** (husband): An easier, less freighted marriage than his first — she asks little of him beyond honesty, which he finds restful after Isolde's careful calculations.
- **Queen Isolde Kaelarion**: Correct and distant rather than warm. Isolde outranks her formally and never lets her forget it; Maris finds the whole hierarchy tedious rather than threatening, which irritates Isolde more than open rivalry would.
- **Princess Nerissa Kaelarion** (daughter): Raising her with as much of Vaelmar's directness as the court will tolerate, determined that her daughter know the sea and the ships that built her mother's house, not only the palace that now holds her.
- **House Vaelmar**: Remains close to her birth house and its interests; functions, more than she would probably admit, as its ear at court.

**Role in Story:** Extends the royal family's alliance web to House Vaelmar and the mainland-facing ports — useful groundwork for when contact with Arcanor becomes active later in the story. Her plainspoken contrast to Isolde also gives the royal household its own version of the Elara/Mira/Sable dynamic already established at Blackspire: two women of very different temperaments and origins, sharing one husband and one uneasy hierarchy.
