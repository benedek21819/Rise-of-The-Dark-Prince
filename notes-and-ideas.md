## Standing Instruction — Cazius
Cazius met Lucian exactly once, briefly, as a masked operative in Chapter 16 ("Veil") — Lucian took the blame for his timing error, asked his name, and shortened it to "Caz" on the spot, ~11 years old at the time. That single encounter is a deliberate early plant: the intent is for the reader to recognize Cazius when he's formally revealed later ("I remember him from long ago"), even though Lucian himself does not consciously track the connection — Guard facelessness plus years of zero further contact mean the name sits dormant in his memory rather than actively known. Aside from that one chapter, Cazius must stay off-page and not actively/consciously known to Lucian until he is formally revealed as Shadow Commander. As of Chapter 51 ("Oversight"), this reveal now has a concrete mechanism: Vesper is doubling the Shadow Guard from 50 to 100 members following Serris's betrayal (Ch.50), splitting it into two named divisions — **Dusk** (the original 50, Vesper's, where Cazius currently serves as Night Captain) and **Dawn** (a new 50 being freshly recruited and trained, a couple of years' work, to be placed under Lucian's direct command once complete). Cazius is projected to rise to Shadow Commander of Dawn by the time it's ready. The reveal chapter should coincide with Dawn's actual completion — likely requiring a future time skip to reach. See `world-building/factions-and-politics.md` ("The Shadow Guard") and `characters/Cazius.md` for full detail.
